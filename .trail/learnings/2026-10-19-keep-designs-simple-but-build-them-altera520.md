## Decided
Prefer the simplest design that still covers the need. Keep information in one place so nothing needs a cross-check, and avoid branching rules for every edge case. Cut proposals that add keys, rules or layers.
Do not pick an option because it is cheapest to build, and do not defer the right design to "later". Design for the real eventual scale, not today's small numbers. The user called out both patterns as wrong.

## Watch out
"Simple" does not mean a stopgap. When the agent flipped between a minimal and a full design, the user objected and asked for one correct design from the start.
