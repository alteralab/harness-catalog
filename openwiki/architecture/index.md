- [Scheduler and operational surface — periodic fetch, healthz, readyz, reindex](scheduler-and-ops.md) - How the registry process starts and stops, how the fetch loop paces itself with exponential backoff, what the two operational routes report and which reason codes an operator will see, and the reindex --accept-rewind command for an intentionally rewound main.
